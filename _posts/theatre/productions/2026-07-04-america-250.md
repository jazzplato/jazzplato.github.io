- Ensemble Member
- Devisor and performer

# Concept
*America "250"* is a devised work by the Yun Theatre Ensemble marking the 250th anniversary of the United States. There was no script: 22 artists spent two weeks building the piece from historical research, oral history, and collective creation, using improvisation, verbatim theatre, and physical theatre.

# Process
The ensemble began devising in late June and presented two performances on July 4th, 2026. A follow-up archival screening and Q&A was held on October 3rd, 2026.
