# My Role
> I performed in **_I'm Sorry, Were You Talking?_** by Daniel Arreola, as one half of a couple in marriage counseling.

# Concept
Instant Noodles is a 24-hour theatre-making festival organized by Pork Filled Productions. More than 50 Asian and Asian American artists are randomly paired, and within 24 hours seven brand-new 10-minute plays are written, rehearsed, and performed. The 2026 theme was "The Model of a Model Minority", and the show was backed by a live band.

# The Play
*I'm Sorry, Were You Talking?* follows a couple in marriage counseling after one of them forgets their anniversary. The session escalates and ends in a begrudging compromise.
